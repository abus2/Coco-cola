ASKED QUESTIONS

What is in the range?

Six flavours: Original Taste, Diet Coke, Zero Sugar, Cherry, Vanilla and Lime, all in a 300 ml can.

Which cans are sugar free?

Diet Coke and Zero Sugar contain no sugar and only 1 kcal per 100 ml. The other four flavours contain 10.6 g of sugars per 100 ml.

Is it fizzy?

Yes, a fine, lively bubble, best served ice cold straight from the can.

Where can I buy it?

Online, in supermarkets and in a selection of bars and gyms.

Who is it for?

Adults. Not recommended for children, pregnant or breastfeeding women, or people sensitive to caffeine (8.7 mg per 100 ml).
